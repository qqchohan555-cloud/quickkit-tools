Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

This is a starter privacy policy for QuickKit Tools. Review and adapt it to your actual operations, analytics, hosting, and advertising setup before publishing.

Information you enter into tools

The calculators and text utilities on this website are designed to process inputs in your browser. The site itself does not intentionally send those tool inputs to a QuickKit server. Avoid entering highly sensitive or confidential information into any online tool.

Hosting logs and third-party services

GitHub Pages may process technical information needed to serve the website. If you enable third-party services such as web analytics, embedded content, or advertising, those providers may collect information according to their own policies and your configuration.

Advertising and cookies

This website may apply for Google AdSense or other advertising services in the future. If advertising is enabled, third parties may use cookies or similar technologies to serve, measure, and personalize ads, subject to applicable consent requirements and provider policies. This section must be updated before enabling ads to accurately describe the services in use.

Your choices

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Depending on your location, additional consent or opt-out options may be required. Do not rely on this starter text as legal advice.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the contact details you add to the Contact page.
